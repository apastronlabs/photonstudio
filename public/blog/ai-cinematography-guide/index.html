Lighting Setup and Prompt Hierarchy

Lighting establishes mood, genre, and emotional weight. Rather than relying on generic adjectives like 'hyperrealistic' or 'photorealistic', describe the exact physical lighting instruments and their color temperatures:

CINEMATIC LIGHTING PROMPT Arri Alexa 65 Setup 
Cinematic medium close-up, anamorphic 2.39:1 aspect ratio, 35mm Arri Alexa 65 sensor. Golden hour low-angle sunlight hitting camera-left as a warm 3200K rim light, cool 5600K ambient shadow fill, subtle volumetric atmospheric dust haze, shallow depth of field, natural skin texture.
